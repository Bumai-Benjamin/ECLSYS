How voting works

Voting happens in three phases. The Electoral Commission moves the election from one phase to the next.

    Before you start

    • You need to be 18 or older.
    • You need your Namibian ID number (11 digits).
    • You need the MetaMask browser extension with a wallet. The wallet is what you vote with.

    The three phases

    1. Registration

    Register your wallet on the Voter registration page: enter your ID number, connect MetaMask, then type the code we email you. You can only register during this phase.

    2. Voting

    On the Voting page, choose a candidate, check your choice, and confirm the transaction in MetaMask. You can vote once, and you can't change your vote afterwards. You get a receipt you can check on the public audit page.

    3. Results

    When voting closes, the totals appear on the Results page. Anyone can re-count the votes on the audit page.

    Your privacy

    Your vote is recorded on the blockchain. Your choice is not stored next to your name in our database.

    In this version a vote is cast from your registered wallet, so someone who can see both the blockchain and the voter register could link the two. Anonymous voting is planned for a later version.
